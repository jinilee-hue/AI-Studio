Wonder Story Lab

An AI story-writing lesson for seven-year-olds · ECP7 Unit 1 Lesson 10

Teacher Teacher view Start a class, take attendance, read each child's finished story, and save the story cards as PDFs. Class 1 Image Maker Look at two scenes, put them in order, answer five questions, and watch the AI draw the scene. Class 2 Story Builder Tell the story of each scene out loud, one sentence at a time, then hand the story card in.

Saving PDFs needs a server. These pages work opened straight from disk, but the teacher's export cannot: reading pixels back out of a canvas that has drawn a local image is blocked unless the page and the image share an origin. Run npx serve . or python -m http.server and open it over http://.
